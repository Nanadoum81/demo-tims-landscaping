FAQ

Questions, answered straight

The things Surprise homeowners ask Tim's Landscaping most often.

How much does an estimate cost?

On-site estimates are free. Send the estimate form or call (623) 308-9675 and we will schedule a time to walk your property, talk through the work, and give you a clear number before anything is agreed to.

What areas do you serve?

Tim's Landscaping is based in Surprise, AZ and serves the West Valley, including Sun City, Sun City West, Peoria, Glendale, El Mirage, Youngtown, and Waddell.

What are your hours?

Our regular hours are Monday through Friday, 6:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Call or send the form any time — we reply during business hours.

Do you trim palm trees?

Yes. Palm trimming is one of our core services — frond and seed-pod removal, shaping, and a full clean-up and haul-away when the trimming is done.

Can you clean up an overgrown yard?

Yes — yard cleanups are one of the most common reasons people call. The crew cuts back overgrowth, clears debris, and hauls everything away, so you get a clean slate. Many cleanup customers then move onto regular maintenance.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Lawn and yard maintenance covers recurring mowing, edging, shrub and hedge trimming, and bed upkeep on a regular schedule, so the yard stays looking cared for week after week.

Will the work stay on budget?

That is the standard customers praise most: you agree on the scope and the number up front, and the crew works to it. If something unexpected comes up, it is discussed with you before — never after.

How do I get started?

Call (623) 308-9675 or send the Request an Estimate form. We will schedule your free on-site estimate, walk the property with you, and lay out exactly what we recommend and what it costs.
